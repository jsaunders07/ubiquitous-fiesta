Living Room
The small living room contains a couch, coffee table, and two
armchairs. Pictures of various cousins adorn the walls along
with two of your aunt's wretched attempts at oil painting. You
can't help but notice that the carpet could use a good
vacuuming.

Doorways lead west to your father's office, up the stairs to your
bedroom, and north to the kitchen.
#
Bedroom
This is your domain and has been since your family moved here
when you were six. A nightstand with a Superman lamp sits next
to your twin bed. Posters of various anime characters cover one
wall. A pile of laundry, slowly but surely attaining sentience,
occupies the far corner.

The door to the attic is in the ceiling above the pile of
clothes. You can  also head back down the stairs to the living room.
#
Office
Your father's office, settled in disarray. Many parts of the room are
overwhelming, and you can't help but think of how boring it must have
been to be stuck up in here all day. The computer still seems to be on,
but without a password it's effectively useless.

The only door in the room leads east back to the living room.
#
Kitchen
The room is as neat as it ever was. All the different tools and
utensils are neatly in their place, except for a few things. A
drawer was left open, and a peek into the fridge shows that much
of the food has gone bad.

There is a door to the west that leads to the Garage, and one
that leads south to the living room.
#
Attic
A very musty room, with cobwebs built up wherever they possibly could
be. There are boxes scattered all across the room with various
miscellaneous items strewn about. Some of it may possibly prove
useful, but it's way too dirty to go rummaging around up here.

The only way to go is back down to the bedroom.
#
Garage
Your family car is still here, but it's being worked on. The car is
jacked up missing half it's tires, and the hood is open revealing that
multiple vital parts are missing. None of the missing items seem to
be present elsewhere in the garage. 

The Garage door is stuck shut, the only way to go is east back to the kitchen.
